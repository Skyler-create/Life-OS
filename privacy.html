LifeOS — Privacy Policy

LifeOS is a personal productivity app built and used by a single person (its author). It is not offered to the public.

Google account data

When connected to Google Calendar, LifeOS reads calendar events and creates or edits events on the author's own calendars. Event data is stored in the author's private database (Supabase) solely to display the calendar inside LifeOS and to plan the author's day. No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

LifeOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the app ("disconnect Google") or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored calendar data is deleted on the next sync after disconnecting.
